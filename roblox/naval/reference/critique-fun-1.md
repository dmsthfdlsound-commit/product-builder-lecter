# 적대적 검증: 재미/리텐션 (1차)

> Player-experience / retention critic — a Roblox player on a cheap Android phone deciding, at second 45, whether to keep playing this or go back to Steal a Brainrot. Not "is this well designed," but "is this fun in session 1 and is there a reason to open it tomorrow."

Evidence base I'm arguing from (Aug 2026): 72–80% of Roblox sessions are mobile and the mobile share is still rising (https://sqmagazine.co.uk/roblox-statistics/). The naval genre on Roblox has a brutally low live ceiling — Navy Simulator has ~35M lifetime visits but a 2026 peak around 220 CCU (https://www.rolimons.com/game/6068707488, https://servicespv.com/en/roblox/stats/navy-simulator-by-sea-military-roblox); the survivors in this genre survive on other players (Tiny Sailors = sandbox with friends, Naval Warfare = nation-vs-nation PvP), not on sailing. The closest thing to this exact game — Arcane Odyssey, a Roblox sailing/ship-combat RPG — went from a 68–80k peak to routinely 1–2k, and its own forum blames repetitive grind, performance, unfinished progression, and "PvP was the only thing keeping the count above 500"; its sailing is openly called "kinda boring" and its wind mechanic is described by players as "the most BORING game experience ever presented to a player" (https://forum.arcaneodyssey.dev/t/what-makes-arcane-odyssey-dead/123940, https://forum.arcaneodyssey.dev/t/sailing-features/153302). Roblox onboarding norms: first reward inside ~60s, every second of non-gameplay in the first 5 minutes costs roughly 2–3% of the new cohort, and "<25% of new users still playing after 5 minutes" is the standard devforum death report (https://rolearn.dev/guidance/first-week-retention-optimization/, https://devforum.roblox.com/t/25-of-new-users-are-still-playing-after-5-minutes/4244172). D1 ≥20% is "good," ≥30% "great," and since June 2026 the recommendation algorithm scores on a 28-day retention window, so a game that bores people in 10 minutes loses its thumbnail advantage (https://about.roblox.com/newsroom/2026/06/optimizing-discovery-great-games-reach-millions-players-roblox, https://bloxg.com/statistics/roblox-retention-benchmarks). Servers under ~40 players read as "empty" to this audience (https://devforum.roblox.com/t/what-is-the-ideal-server-size-social-slots-to-do-for-my-game/2319127).

THE SINGLE BIGGEST FUN RISK: the first 90 seconds are a sailing tutorial, not a fight — and the document's own kill-switch (S5, the 3-minute handle test) is rigged to pass it. 7 hand-picked testers, one of them the dev's friend, physically present, asked to hold a wheel for 3 minutes, will not quit; an anonymous 12-year-old on an A14 with a Blox Fruits tab open quits at 40 seconds. S5 measures "is this a pleasant toy," not "would you choose this over what's on the Discover page." It will PASS, hand over false confidence, and the project will discover the real answer at W16 when D1 comes back at 12%.
THE FIX: invert the cold open, and re-run S5 against strangers. New player spawns already under fire — a wounded Lv.4 schooner 110 studs off the port beam, crew screaming "THEY'RE ON US, FIRE!", the two broadside buttons pulsing. First cannon volley by second 10, first hull-breach VFX by second 12, first sink by second 60–90, first boarding reward menu inside 3 minutes, THEN the ocean opens and free sailing is the reward rather than the tax. Change the W10 gate from "first sink ≤ 6 min" to "first broadside hit ≤ 20s, first sink ≤ 100s." And re-spec S5: unlisted place link posted publicly to 20+ strangers, metric = median time-to-quit with no facilitator in the room, PASS = median ≥ 3 min. That costs one session and is the only version of the test that means anything.

**판정: risky**

## 🚫 Sailing-first cold open: the first 5 minutes are a handling tutorial and the W10 gate literally targets 'first sink ≤ 6 minutes'
**실패 시나리오**: Mobile player (80% of sessions) taps the icon, gets water, a horizon, and two gear buttons with no on-screen objective — the doc removed tutorial UI from the vertical slice and replaced it with 'verbal guidance,' which does not exist for a real player. At ~2-3% cohort loss per second of non-gameplay, a 6-minute time-to-first-kill means the majority of new users never see the game's actual identity (chip-chip-pop-snipe). D1 lands nowhere near the 25% W16 gate, and because the June 2026 algorithm scores on a 28-day retention window, the game never gets impressions to recover with. Worse, the failure is invisible until W16 because S5 tested friends, not strangers.

**처방**: Scripted ambush cold open: spawn mid-combat against a pre-damaged Lv.4 schooner at 110 studs, broadside buttons pulsing, crew barking the instruction. First volley ≤10s, first sink ≤100s, first boarding reward menu ≤3min, free sailing unlocked after. Re-gate W10 on 'first broadside hit ≤20s' instead of 'first sink ≤6min.' Re-spec S5 as a public unlisted-link test with 20+ strangers, PASS = median time-to-quit ≥3min, no facilitator present.

## 🚫 v1 contains no other humans. No PvP, no co-op, no guild; 2-seat crewing is scheduled for P6 (W27, month 7). Server cap is 8.
**실패 시나리오**: Every Roblox naval game that retains anyone retains them socially — Tiny Sailors (sandbox with friends), Naval Warfare (nation PvP) — and Arcane Odyssey's own community says PvP was the only thing holding its count above 500. With a genre CCU ceiling in the low hundreds, an 8-slot server will hold 2-3 humans scattered across a 1000+ stud streaming radius, so a player literally never sees another ship with a nameplate. A kid finishes a good session, wants to tell a friend, and there is nothing to invite them INTO — no shared ship, no shared objective, no leaderboard, no killfeed. That is a D7 of ~4%, not 10%, and it makes the P4/P5 gates unreachable no matter how good the handling feels. The doc itself calls co-op the biggest differentiator and the thing Black Flag cut — then schedules it last.

**처방**: Move the 2-seat ship (helm + gunner) into the vertical slice, not P6. The doc already admits it is 'nearly free' if S2 passes L1/L2, so make it the first thing built after S2 resolves and pay for it by cutting a hull class and a region from P3. Add in P2: a server-wide killfeed and a shared bounty target ('the Ironjaw is hunting THIS server — 3 captains needed'), friend-invite that drops the friend onto your deck as gunner, and raise the server cap so the ocean has visible nameplates even if only 6 of 8 slots fill.

## 🚫 Perfect brace = 0.25s input window, telegraphed by an orange glow on a distant enemy model, on a phone, on a game whose own netcode spec accepts 350ms perceived round-trip
**실패 시나리오**: The numbers contradict each other: S4 declares 350ms fire-to-damage-feedback acceptable and forces 250ms replication lag in every test, then the defense layer asks for a 250ms reaction window keyed off an event rendered on an enemy ship that at 200+ studs is (per S3's own fallback) a single-part silhouette with no visible gunports. A mobile player will never land a perfect brace, will conclude brace is a panic button, and the entire skill ceiling of the defensive half of combat silently disappears — while they blame 'input lag' in the reviews. Meanwhile a PC player with 40ms ping lands it consistently, so the -80% vs -45% gap becomes a platform-inequity balance problem on a platform that is 80% mobile.

**처방**: Widen perfect window to 0.40-0.45s, make brace timing client-authoritative (it is a damage-reduction buff — rate-limit it and validate frequency, not timing; the cheat surface is negligible versus the fun surface). Move the telegraph off the enemy mesh into screen space: a HUD arc on the threatened side that fills over 0.9s plus a rising audio cue and a controller-free 'flash the brace button' tell, so it is readable at any range and at any LOD. Test the window on the cheap Android with 250ms forced lag before locking the number.

## 🚫 Rogue wave: side impact = ×3.0 damage, but the doc's own turn rates make alignment physically impossible at speed
**실패 시나리오**: From the doc's own table: 90° of turn takes ~9s at gear2 (10°/s) and ~16s at gear3 (5.5°/s). The minimap ping lead time is unspecified. If the warning is under ~12s the wave is unavoidable damage, which reads to a player not as 'the sea is a character' but as 'the game reached in and took 300 HP.' Worse, the only reliable counter is dropping to gear0 to pivot at 22°/s, i.e. coming to a dead stop — which mid-fight is suicide. So the mechanic's optimal play is 'stop fighting,' and its common experience is 'unfair chunk damage.' New players eat a ×3.0 side hit during their first engagement, cross a segment boundary, lose the fight, and quit.

**처방**: Warning lead ≥12s at gear3 with a horizon-line VFX (not only a minimap ping — nobody watches a minimap on a phone mid-fight), cap the side multiplier at ×2.0, and hard-clamp a single wave so it can never cross a segment boundary. Then add an upside: a correctly-aligned bow-on ride gives a 3s speed surge plus a crew cheer, so the wave is an opportunity you learn to want, not a tax you learn to dread.

## 🚫 The alpha build (W16, where D1/D7 are first measured) has zero return hooks — contracts, idle fleet, seasons are all P4/P6
**실패 시나리오**: At W16 the game is: sail, fight, upgrade with a power curve deliberately capped at 2.5x floor-to-ceiling. There is no daily, no login streak, no rotating objective, no timed event, no idle income, nothing on a 24-hour clock. A tester plays two good sessions and simply forgets it exists — not because sailing is bad, but because nothing pinged. D1 ≥25% and 14-minute sessions will fail. Then the doc's own rule ('never respond to a gate miss with content') sends the dev into repeated first-5-minutes redesigns while the actual hole is 'there is nothing to come back for,' and the schedule bleeds a month. Compounding it: a 2.5x total power curve on an audience trained by Blox Fruits on 20-100x curves means each upgrade purchase feels like nothing happened — correct for region-gradient design, poisonous for perceived progress.

**처방**: Pull ONE return hook into P2/P3: a 3-slot daily contract board that reuses verbs already implemented (sink 3 schooners / complete 1 boarding / pop 2 weak points) with a 24h reset and a visible streak counter. Zero new systems, ~1 session. Pay for it by cutting the third hull class and the third region from P3. And since raw power must stay flat, make progress LEGIBLE another way: every upgrade must change something you can see or hear in one shot (muzzle flash size, reload cadence, a new crew animation, hull silhouette), plus an always-visible 'next unlock: 1 more sinking' bar — Roblox players need the counter on screen at all times.

## 🚫 Loss spiral: 500R repair + up to 30% material loss on sinking + 2-5 crew lost per boarding + no crew means boarding (and therefore free healing) is locked
**실패 시나리오**: A player who loses one fight is simultaneously poorer, short on crew, unable to board, and therefore unable to heal for free — so they must go win a fight in a damaged ship with a thin crew. That is the classic mid-core death spiral, and this audience does not grind out of a hole; they close the app. The doc's own insurance principle ('loss must not be theft') is violated by its own economy table, because these four penalties stack on the same bad session. The 12-14 year old testers in the W10 protocol will hit this and the 'want to play again' metric will read as a fun problem when it is an economy problem.

**처방**: In v1: repair is FREE at port (charge Reals only for upgrades — the upgrade wall is your economy, not a toll on failure), crew auto-replenishes free up to a floor of 12 so boarding is never locked out, and hard-guarantee that losing a fight never reduces your ability to earn from the next one. Keep the material loss cap but exempt the first sinking of each session. Losing should cost you TIME and pride, never capability.

## 🚫 The pirate-captain fantasy is delivered entirely as a vehicle, and the S2-L3 fallback removes the body from the game
**실패 시나리오**: On Roblox, identity is avatar-shaped — that is what these players buy, screenshot, and show off. This game's cosmetics are sail textures, hull tints, figureheads, wake colors. If S2 degrades to L3 (a 6-second boarding cutscene plus a reward menu), the player never once inhabits a pirate: they drive a boat, watch a cutscene, and pick a button. 'I felt like a pirate captain' fails in session 1, the cosmetic shop's conversion assumption (1.5%) fails at P4 because nobody buys a wake color, and the marketing screenshot ('a stranger docked with a gilded sail') is a picture of a boat, not of a person.

**처방**: Guarantee one avatar-visible payoff in v1 regardless of how S2 resolves: your own captain stands visibly at the wheel with idle/brace/point animations that the gear camera can actually see, and ship cosmetics ship alongside 3-4 captain-wearable items (hat, coat, spyglass) that render in third person and while docked at port. Cheap, buys the fantasy, and gives the cosmetic economy something a 13-year-old actually wants to own.

## 🚫 The arena pivot is filed as failure fallback #3 when it is plausibly the stronger v1 product
**실패 시나리오**: The doc only reaches for '400x400 studs, 5v5 gunnery arena' if the cheap Android misses 30fps. But on the merits for this audience the arena version wins on every fun axis this critique is worried about: zero travel tax, humans guaranteed in every match, a 7-minute session shape that mobile naval already proved works (World of Warships Blitz runs ~7-8 minute battles), no streaming/AI/region cost, natural friend-invite, natural repeat play. Treating it purely as a performance concession means the decision gets made on frame timings instead of on retention, and the 30-week open-world plan proceeds by default because the technical gate happened to pass.

**처방**: Build the arena mode as an explicit A/B at the P2 gate, not as a fallback. Same ship code, same combat, one 400x400 map, 4v4, 7-minute rounds. Give strangers both builds and compare median session length and next-day return. If the arena wins, ship the arena first and treat the open ocean as content built on a live, retaining game — which also fixes blockers 1, 2 and 5 in one move.

## ✂️ 컷 권고
- Mortar (3.5s flight time) — cut from v1 entirely, keep only the direct-fire chase cannon. A 3.5s time-of-flight lead shot against a 102 studs/s target, aimed with a thumb on a 6-inch screen at 250ms latency, is a miss generator: the doc's own S4 pass bar (18/20 shots landing within 3 studs of the predicted circle) is a netcode success criterion, not a fun criterion. Even if the netcode passes, the player experience is 'I fired, waited 3.5 seconds, and nothing happened.' Reclaims a spike session and removes the P4 contract line that exists to teach it.
- Wind speed multiplier — cut the multiplier, keep the visuals (sail billow, flag direction, spray). Do NOT spend session W4-2 A/B testing it. Wind that fights your heading is the single most-hated mechanic in the one directly comparable Roblox product (Arcane Odyssey players call it the most boring experience in the game and ask to be allowed to AFK through it). This is a known landmine with public evidence; testing it is spending a session to rediscover it.
- Idle fleet / Kenway-style passive income (P4) — defer past launch. It is a second economy with its own UI, save schema, and balance surface that adds nothing to the moment-to-moment fun, and it competes for the same 'reason to log in tomorrow' slot that a 1-session daily contract board fills better and 10x cheaper. Its budget is the down payment on 2-seat co-op.
- Third hull class and third region at P3 → ship 2 hulls, 2 regions. More content does not fix retention (the doc's own gate rule says so) and the alpha's real deficiency is a return hook and a second player, not more boats. Frees ~1.5 weeks.
- 15 contract types at P4 → 5 contracts, pulled forward to P3. Ten of the fifteen are variance on the same verbs; the value is the daily reset and the streak counter, not the catalogue size.
- Rogue wave as a damage mechanic in v1 if the ≥12s warning + segment-clamp cannot be delivered cleanly. Ship it as spectacle-and-speed-boost only. The doc claims one rogue wave captures 90% of 'the sea is a character' — true, but only if the wave is readable; an unreadable wave captures 100% of 'this game is unfair.'
- Localization to 4 languages before launch — English-first, string table in place, translate after the retention gates pass. Localizing a game nobody returns to is polishing a corpse.
- Camera-shake intensity settings UI at W2-5 — real accessibility win, wrong week. Ship it at P3 with the rest of the settings menu; W2 sessions belong to crew and audio, which are load-bearing for the empty-ocean problem.

## 🏛️ 반드시 지킬 것
- Crew over particles (W2, 8-14 crew × 5 states + 8 barks). This is the best single call in the document and it is the direct antidote to the empty-ocean problem: a solo player on a quiet sea is alone, but a solo player with eight men hauling rope and shouting 'SAIL HO, OFF THE PORT BOW!' is a captain with a company. Do not cut a single crew state to save time — cut a hull class instead. Barks are also your tutorial: every instruction the removed tutorial UI would have given should come out of a crewman's mouth.
- Shanty ducking on combat entry — the 0.8s fade to near-silence when the fight starts. This is a 15-second-clip-quality emotional beat that costs almost nothing, and silence is the cheapest tension generator in games. Keep the sudden-quiet, and add the reverse: the shanty crashing back in when the enemy sinks is your victory fanfare, free.
- Gear-linked camera (distance 1.0-1.8x, FOV 70-86, asymmetric 1.2s/0.6s ramp) and the gear0 pivot as an undiscovered skill. This is the actual product differentiator, it is the free TikTok/Shorts marketing asset, and the 'at least 3 of 7 testers discover the gear0 pivot with no explanation' criterion is the one genuinely well-designed test in the document. Keep the discovery bar.
- Segment HP ratchet with an 8s regen that stops at the current segment ceiling. The anticipatory dread of watching healing halt mid-bar is real and it reads on a phone — provided the gauge is large, top-center, and the halt has a distinct mechanical sound. Do not shrink this UI for screen real estate.
- Cosmetic-only monetization, no power sales, no full-loot PvP, 30% loss cap with insurance and a revenge contract. Correct for this audience and correct ethically; the 2026 discovery algorithm's 28-day retention weighting rewards exactly this over a whale-milking economy. Hold the line on it even when the P4 revenue gate misses.
- Hard device gates on a real Galaxy A14 / iPhone SE2 with 'Studio numbers are not evidence' as an explicit rule, plus the 250ms forced replication lag in every integration test. Almost no solo Roblox dev does this and it is why most mid-core Roblox games are unplayable for their own majority platform. Extend the same rule to input: every combat window (brace, weak-point shot, boarding prompt) gets validated with a thumb on that A14 before its number is locked.
- The pre-committed kill criteria and the arena pivot existing at all. Writing the sunk-cost defense down in advance is the most professionally mature thing in this plan. The only change needed is promoting the arena from 'performance fallback' to 'fun A/B candidate' (see blockers).
- WIP limit of 1, the 3-session spike tripwire, and 'never answer a missed gate with more content.' These rules are what will keep a 30-week solo project alive. They are also why blocker 5 matters so much: with content-additions forbidden, the return hook has to be scheduled deliberately and early, or the rule will forbid the fix at exactly the moment it is needed.
