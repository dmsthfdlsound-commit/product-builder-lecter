# 로블록스 해양 게임 경쟁 지형 (2026.8)

> Competitive landscape: pirate / naval / sea games on Roblox (as of August 2026)

## There is NO large dedicated naval-combat game on Roblox. The biggest pure-naval title runs ~1,000 CCU.
Naval Warfare (created Aug 2018) has accumulated ~534M lifetime visits but currently sits around 1,009 concurrent players. Sail and Sink Simulator (Jan 2024, ~79M visits) sits around 1,819 CCU. Navy Simulator (Dec 2020, ~36M visits) is at roughly 129–220 CCU with a tracked record of only ~220 simultaneous (May 2026). Galleons, a legacy classic, has ~22.6M visits and 373k favorites but negligible live population. Numbers are from stat-aggregator snapshots and fluctuate; treat as order-of-magnitude.

**→ 시사점**: The naval-combat genre on Roblox is dead weight at the top, not a fortress. We would not be fighting an incumbent with 200k CCU — we'd be fighting a vacuum. The genre label 'Naval' is effectively unclaimed.

## Arcane Odyssey is the flagship pirate/naval RPG and it has collapsed twice — from 40–80k at launch to under 2k, then 24k at Full Release to ~7k in weeks.
AO (Vetex) has ~153.6M visits, 92.4% rating, ~875k favorites, avg session ~33 min. Community reports: 40,000+ at Feb 2023 early-access launch with peaks cited as high as 68–80k; two years later it 'rarely breaks 2,000.' Full Release (Dec 26, 2025) spiked to ~24k, and a Jan 2026 forum thread reports the decline to ~7k. Mid-2026 snapshots show ~800 concurrent. Retention, not acquisition, is the failure.

**→ 시사점**: A well-made, high-rated pirate RPG with real ship systems still cannot hold players. Retention design (not art or scope) is the thing to solve. Assume our first-week spike is easy and our week-4 number is the whole battle.

## The single most repeated complaint across every sea game is that SAILING ITSELF IS BORING.
Arcane Odyssey players: 'Sailing is kinda boring, especially compared to Sea of Thieves or even Shipping Lanes, there's just no good wave action'; 'the sitting there watching the waves roll by has just gotten boring'; travel between islands described as 'awful.' Worse, AO's wind mechanic actively punishes AFK — you must 'babysit your ship in the most BORING game experience' or be turned around entirely. Blox Fruits, Fisch, and Sailor Piece all sidestep this by making the boat a disposable taxi rather than a vehicle you inhabit.

**→ 시사점**: Travel time is the #1 churn driver. Either compress voyages to <60s of dead time, or make the voyage the content (an event/hazard/decision every 20–40 seconds). Do NOT ship a realistic wind/tacking model that demands attention without giving reward — that combination is the worst of both worlds.

## Sailor Piece is the real 2026 breakout in the sea space — peak ~743.7k CCU — and it is an anime grind RPG, not a sailing game.
Launched Nov 17, 2025 by Shadowrise Devs. Tracked historical record ~743.7k simultaneous players on April 19, 2026; routinely 300–416k, regularly outpacing Blox Fruits at peak. Estimated ~$2.13M/month in Robux revenue (third-party estimate, treat as rough). Boats exist purely as upgradable inter-island transport (upgrade speed and durability); the loop is boss farming, drops, Haki/fighting-style/clan/trait/race progression, Sea 1 → Sea 2 (level cap 16,000). Monetization: Race Reroll (2,500 R$), Stat Reset, Teleport, XP Boost, Inventory Expansion, Fruit Storage; markets itself as 'fully playable for free' and community broadly agrees — deliberately positioned against Blox Fruits' heavier monetization.

**→ 시사점**: Proof that the sea SETTING can carry 700k+ CCU on Roblox — but only wrapped around a legible anime power-progression loop. If our game is a naval sim first and a progression RPG second, we are choosing the 1k-CCU bracket. Also: 'more generous than the incumbent' is a proven wedge on this platform.

## Blox Fruits (~245–288k CCU, ~60–62.4B visits) treats ships as consumable furniture, and players are fine with that.
Boats are bought from docks; advice is to never use one under 2,000 HP because sea events destroy them. Boats despawn if nobody is in the driver's seat or if you exit a Dimensional Rift off-seat, forcing a rebuy. Players beat down their own boat to reclaim nails and rebuild after a portal. Sea events (Terrorshark, Rumbling Waters, Haunted Ship Raid, Mirage Island, Kitsune Island, Leviathan, Kraken) are what makes Third Sea sailing tolerable — 'less like travel and more like a planned hunt.' Update 29 'Control' launched Dec 23, 2025.

**→ 시사점**: Blox Fruits accidentally found the answer to boring sailing: dense, high-value random encounters that turn transit into a hunt. Steal this directly — a table of sea events with real loot, spawning on a tight cadence. Also note nobody forms an emotional bond with a disposable boat; if we want ship attachment we must make the ship persistent and unloseable.

## Build a Boat for Treasure is the actual king of 'ships' on Roblox — ~5.07B visits, 10–21k CCU — and it is a BUILDING game, not a sailing game.
Chillz Studios, 2016. 5.07B+ visits as of June 30, 2026; ~10,000–20,000 steady CCU with snapshots up to ~21,386 (around rank #50 platform-wide). Loop: build a block boat in a free-form editor, run it through an obstacle-course river, earn gold, buy better blocks, repeat. Monetization: gold/blocks sold for Robux, gamepasses from 10 R$ up to 100,000 R$, save slots, morphs; the Gold Multiplier (~500 R$) is the community-agreed best buy because it compounds every run. Complaints are almost entirely physics/performance: mass unanchored blocks (e.g. spike traps) cause 'massive lag,' launching causes severe FPS/ping spikes, loose blocks clip through hulls and ruin multi-part designs, boats get slower with more seated players and slower still with unseated ones. 'Lag machine' tutorials are a whole content genre.

**→ 시사점**: Free-form ship building is the single most proven monetizable mechanic in this space, and its ceiling is Roblox physics, not player interest. If we offer BABFT-grade build freedom with combat that BABFT lacks, that is a genuinely empty quadrant — but we must design the build system around a strict part budget from day one.

## Arcane Odyssey's ship system is the most sophisticated on the platform: tiered hulls gated by level AND gold, with slot-based customization and fleets.
Progression: Sailboat → Yacht → Caravel (1,250 galleons, L25) → Ketch (3,000 galleons, L75, 2500 durability / 45% stability / 70 turning / 60 speed / 65% resilience) → Brig (20,000 galleons, L120, only ship with a Captain's Quarters). Only Caravel and Brig can mount rams; Caravel fires up to 6 cannons. Ships are customized in a Shipyard menu with slots for sails, hulls, rams, cannons and misc parts. Captured ships carry durability titles (e.g. 'Fortified Pirate Caravel'). Player Fleets and Clan 'Naval Wars' (ship PvP on open ocean) exist on top.

**→ 시사점**: This is the design bar for ship depth and it is genuinely good — copying its tier+slot structure is safe. But note the lesson: AO built the deepest ship system on Roblox and still bled players, because depth of ships ≠ reason to log in tomorrow.

## Ship-vs-ship COMBAT is broken or shallow in every game that attempts it — including the flagship.
Arcane Odyssey forum reports: siege weapons work against NPC ships but not player ships, with direct hits sometimes dealing zero damage; ramming a ship that hasn't aggro'd yet deals ~200 instead of the expected ~2,000 from a full-speed pulse-magic ram; ramming is unreliable, players 'miss by tiny amounts and slide off.' Grand Piece Online ship sailing is 'very buggy' — players fling or drown while moving on a ship, and moves with long endlag leave the character in place while the ship sails out from under them. Naval Warfare has had no new ships or aircraft for roughly six years, one map and one fixed mode, so 'the meta is stuck.'

**→ 시사점**: Nobody on Roblox has shipped ship-vs-ship combat that actually feels good. This is the clearest technical/design moat available: if broadsides, boarding and ramming land reliably and readably, we beat every competitor on the one axis the genre is named after.

## Open-ocean exploration content consistently turns into a hated grind — Arcane Odyssey's 'Dark Sea' is the case study.
The Dark Sea is AO's signature endless-ocean exploration zone and it generated thread after thread: 'The Dark Sea Update Sucks,' 'Dark sea is boring,' 'How the dark sea turned out very tedious (rant),' 'Ways to make the Dark Sea less miserable,' 'Does the Dark Sea feel lacking or boring to you?' Specific gripes: farming dark sealed chests is 'very repetitive'; most islands are 'insanely tall' so climbing for a chest isn't worth it; players get 'almost nothing every island'; the water-poisoning/insanity mechanics are called unfair, RNG-dependent and unfun; plus server lag and overtuned NPCs.

**→ 시사점**: Procedural open ocean is a trap: players read empty water as wasted time, and RNG loot with high friction reads as punishment. If we build an exploration sea, every voyage must have a guaranteed floor of reward and a hazard system that is a skill test, not a stat drain.

## Open-world PvP with bounty hunting is the second-biggest churn driver in Arcane Odyssey.
Thread titles include 'I guess PvP is as toxic as it's ever been,' 'Who else hates bounty hunters,' and '[suggestion] Fix PVP and bounty hunting, make it fair for non PVP players.' Reported issues: tryhards hunt low-bounty players instead of harder high-fame targets; players report being attacked with PvP toggled off and losing large sums to both death and execution penalties; jail time after being jumped; and inversely, low-level players can steal ships, cargo and chests from high-level players with no risk of reprisal. Structurally, joining a Faction keeps PvP off until 250,000 fame/bounty, but creating or joining a Clan or Faction Division forces PvP ON permanently and irreversibly.

**→ 시사점**: Roblox's audience will not tolerate involuntary PvP with permanent loss. Design PvP as consented, bracketed by power level, and with capped downside — and never make an endgame social system (clans) silently force-enable it.

## Multi-crew ships are an unsolved ENGINEERING problem on Roblox, which is why nobody has shipped a Sea of Thieves clone.
Roblox devforum threads recur on exactly this: 'How to handle NetworkOwner/movement on a crewed ship with multiple players?', 'Multiple Network Owners on a Single Ship?', 'Players do not move with ship.' Network ownership of a physics assembly goes to one client, so other crew members desync, fling, or drown. Constraint-based ship rigs (PlaneConstraint for buoyancy, LinearVelocity/AngularVelocity for motion, AlignOrientation to stay upright, HingeConstraint for banking) are reported to 'hog server resources.' Terrain water is inadequate, so serious projects hand-build skinned-mesh/FFT oceans with LOD and sync waves via workspace:GetServerTimeNow() — one published solution runs ~0.05ms with 9 planes. The most-hyped Roblox pirate project, 'High Seas' by Infinite_Visions (8 devlogs, endless ocean, rope swinging, ship build system, Kraken, huge archipelago), reached a limited beta in Jan 2022 and then went quiet.

**→ 시사점**: Budget real engineering time for the ocean + boarding + crew-sync layer before content. Conversely, this difficulty IS the moat — if we solve locally-simulated ships with server-authoritative positions, we own a capability the whole genre lacks. And do not assume a beautiful ship demo equals a shippable game; High Seas is the cautionary tale.

## Arcane Odyssey's real-money (non-Robux) subscription drew community backlash — a monetization landmine to avoid.
AO monetizes mostly cosmetically: 8 standalone game passes, 7 animation packs, 8 magic circles, plus extra save files and loadouts that are convenience-only, and the community broadly agrees it is not pay-to-win. But Vetex added monthly subscriptions billed in real money by credit card, 'SPECIFICALLY not robux,' around $5/month, granting a title and a ship vanity item — and the community reaction in the searched threads is negative and confused. If the subscription lapses, the items are revoked.

**→ 시사점**: Follow AO's cosmetic-first, no-P2W stance (it's the reason for its 92.4% rating) but monetize strictly inside Robux. Off-platform billing on a Roblox game reads as a betrayal to this audience, and revocable-on-lapse items compound the resentment.

## Fisch shows the winning boat model for a mass-market Roblox audience: dozens of tiered boats with four legible stats, as pure utility.
Released Oct 5, 2024; ~47,000–65,000 CCU and 5B+ visits. Boats let players travel between islands, reach fishing spots, complete quests and join limited-time events. Every boat has exactly four stats — Speed, Steering, Acceleration, Seats — across dozens of vessels from starter rowboats to luxury ships, event-exclusives and high-speed endgame rides. The boat is never the challenge; it's the key to content.

**→ 시사점**: Four readable stats and a long, collectible tier ladder beats a simulation-grade rig for retention and monetization. Ship acquisition is a collection loop — that's the drip we should build, with event-exclusive hulls as the live-ops lever.

## Progression grind and zero replayability are the shared endgame failure of every sea RPG here.
Arcane Odyssey: a full playthrough is ~12–18 hours to max level and 'everything after is busywork'; build progression means 'repeating the same motions... with different stat builds'; a dedicated thread argues 'AO has probably some of the worst replayability I've seen on a roblox game'; and because data doesn't transfer between save files, an alt is 'an almost unplayable slog.' Blox Fruits: quest cycles are 'one quest, finish it, gain exp, do the same thing for 20-30 minutes, then move on'; weeks to max level; PvP 'comes down to either paying robux or being lucky enough to roll rare fruits.' Sailor Piece: players report boredom because too much is AFK-able, making the grind feel unearned, and the community's own proposed fix is a PvP update.

**→ 시사점**: Three separate failure modes to design against: (1) content that ends at ~15h with no loop after, (2) grind that can be automated/AFK'd and therefore feels worthless, (3) account-bound progress that kills replay. Our answer should be a repeatable, skill-expressive voyage loop with account-wide unlocks and a horizontal (not vertical) endgame.

## ⛔ 하드 제약
- Roblox network ownership is per physics assembly and single-client — a ship carrying multiple players cannot be authoritatively simulated by all of them. Multi-crew ships WILL desync, fling, or drown players unless the ship is locally simulated on every client with server-authoritative transform reconciliation. Recurring unsolved devforum topic; budget this as core engineering, not polish.
- Roblox Terrain water is not viable for a large playable ocean. A shippable naval game requires a custom skinned-mesh / FFT ocean with LOD and client-side wave reconstruction synced off workspace:GetServerTimeNow(). Achievable (published solutions run ~0.05ms with ~9 planes) but it means buoyancy, swimming, splash, and boarding all become bespoke systems.
- Constraint-driven ship rigs (PlaneConstraint buoyancy + LinearVelocity/AngularVelocity + AlignOrientation + HingeConstraint banking) measurably hog server CPU at pirate-ship scale. Large ships must be locally rendered/simulated, not server-physics-driven.
- Unanchored part counts are a hard performance ceiling for free-form ship building. Build a Boat for Treasure — the largest game in this space — has an entire community content genre around deliberately crashing servers with block masses; launching a large boat causes severe FPS/ping spikes and loose blocks clip through hulls. Any free-form builder needs a strict part budget, welded-assembly baking, and collision simplification from day one.
- Physics-heavy sea games run small servers. Arcane Odyssey is tracked at ~12 max players per server. A 50–100 player open naval battlefield is not economically or technically realistic; design for roughly 10–30 players per shard and fake scale with NPC fleets.
- Roblox's audience skews mobile/tablet and to short sessions (Arcane Odyssey averages ~33 minutes even as a deep RPG). Multi-station ship controls that assume keyboard + voice comms + a committed 90-minute session will not convert. Every crew role needs a one-touch, solo-viable fallback (NPC crew or automation).
- Real-money billing outside Robux is a reputational cost, not just a fee saving. Arcane Odyssey's credit-card ~$5/month subscription (items revoked on lapse) generated confusion and backlash in a community that otherwise praises its monetization. Monetize inside Robux.
- Involuntary open-world PvP with meaningful loss is not tolerated by this audience — AO's bounty-hunting system with death plus execution penalties, jail time, and clan membership silently force-enabling PvP permanently is one of its most-cited churn drivers. Consented, power-bracketed, capped-downside PvP only.
- Procedurally generated open ocean cannot carry content load. AO's Dark Sea — the most ambitious open-sea exploration zone on the platform — is its most complained-about feature (repetitive chest farming, near-empty islands, RNG-gated rewards, water-poisoning attrition, server lag). Handcrafted or guaranteed-reward voyage content only.
- The 'Naval' genre tag itself delivers almost no discovery traffic — every game filed under it sits in the hundreds-to-low-thousands CCU. Acquisition must come from a recognizable hook (progression RPG, building, survival run, IP-adjacent aesthetic), not from the naval category.

## 💡 기회
- Ship-vs-ship combat that actually WORKS is unclaimed. Arcane Odyssey — the genre flagship — has siege weapons that deal zero damage to player ships, ramming that under-delivers 10x and slides off targets, and Grand Piece Online flings players off moving decks. Readable, reliable broadsides + ramming + boarding is a differentiator on the exact axis the genre is named after.
- BABFT-grade build freedom + real combat is an empty quadrant. Build a Boat for Treasure owns free-form ship building (5.07B visits) with no combat; Arcane Odyssey owns ship combat with only slot-based customization. Nobody has combined 'the boat I designed' with 'the boat I fight in' — and player-authored ships are the most proven monetization surface in this space (premium blocks, gold multipliers, save slots).
- Solo-viable crewing. Every crew-ship game on Roblox assumes coordinated humans and dies to Roblox's actual social reality (randoms, short sessions, mobile). NPC crew that mans stations, plus one-button station takeover, converts the fantasy without the dependency — and no competitor has solved it.
- 400,000+ members sit in naval/maritime roleplay GROUPS (Naval Roleplay Community ~241.7k, Maritime Roleplay Community ~175.6k) with no good game to play. Captain identity, crew ranks, ship names, uniforms and fleet hierarchy are cheap features that convert a pre-assembled audience the current games ignore.
- Make the voyage the content instead of the tax. Blox Fruits accidentally solved boring sailing with dense sea events (Terrorshark, Rumbling Waters, Kraken, Leviathan, Mirage Island) that turn transit into 'a planned hunt.' No dedicated naval game has a proper sea-event table — it is the single highest-leverage fix to the genre's #1 complaint.
- Directional voyage beats open sandbox. Rusty Rafts went 0 → ~20k peak CCU in weeks (Feb 2026 launch, 86.1% rating) on 'build a raft, defend it, sail north to a rumored safe haven' — a destination and a threat, with a fraction of AO's content. Dead Sails proved the run-based naval voyage acquires (39.8M visits) but dies without meta-progression; build the between-runs layer first and this format is wide open.
- Undercut incumbents on generosity. Sailor Piece took ~743.7k peak CCU partly by positioning as 'fully playable for free' against Blox Fruits' P2W reputation ('grind 200 hours or pay robux'). Cosmetic-and-convenience-only monetization, entirely in Robux, is both the community-praised model (AO holds a 92.4% rating on it) and a live marketing wedge.
- Legacy naval games are abandoned and harvestable. Naval Warfare has ~534M lifetime visits, no new ships or aircraft in ~6 years, one map, one mode, a frozen meta, and gamepass-gated vessels. Half a billion visits' worth of demonstrated appetite is sitting behind a game nobody is updating.
- Horizontal endgame instead of a 15-hour wall. AO finishes in ~12–18h with 'busywork' after and no cross-save transfer; Blox Fruits is weeks of identical 20–30 minute quest cycles; Sailor Piece is criticized for being AFK-able. A repeatable, skill-expressive voyage with account-wide unlocks and side-grade ships (not stat ladders) targets the exact hole all three leave.
- Ship acquisition as a collection loop. Fisch holds ~47–65k CCU with dozens of boats described by just four legible stats (Speed, Steering, Acceleration, Seats) plus event-exclusive hulls. A long tiered ship ladder with limited-time vessels is a proven retention and live-ops lever that no pirate-combat game on Roblox currently runs.
